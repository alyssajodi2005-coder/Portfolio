THE PERSON BEHIND THE PIXELS

Hi, I’m Alyssa.

A design mind. An engineering heart.

I’m a Computer Science student at The City College of New York, pursuing a Mathematics minor and expecting to graduate in 2027. I bring design and engineering together to organize complex information and build clear, usable interfaces.

As Sankofa subsite lead on the Melanate project, I created wireframes and page layouts, organized content for CMS integration, and iterated with designers, developers, and the nonprofit client. For Our Neighborhood Mission, I independently built a responsive website in HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap.

I’m interested in product design, frontend and full-stack development, AI and machine learning, and accessible technology for nonprofit and community-focused organizations.

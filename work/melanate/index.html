01

The overview

A little context.

Melanate supports Black women and gender-expansive professionals in philanthropy, cultivating restoration, equity, and leadership among those shaping how money moves in the South. Through Develop for Good, our team helped create a visually refreshed, strategically organized website with clearer educational pathways and interactive experiences for Sankofa. I led design for the Sankofa subsite and contributed to the main homepage.

Explore the live website
02

The challenge

The problem worth solving.

Communicate Melanate’s mission and educational content through a cohesive website, while giving Sankofa room for interactive storytelling. The work connected page layouts, content organization, and implementation in Wix Harmony, with feedback from the nonprofit client throughout the process.

Design goals & considerations

  1. Make Melanate’s mission and educational content easier to navigate.
  2. Create an engaging Sankofa experience through timeline cards and visual storytelling.
  3. Organize structured content for CMS integration and translate designs into Wix Harmony.
↳

My responsibilities

My part in a shared effort.

I led design work for the Sankofa subsite and contributed to the main homepage. My responsibilities included wireframes and page layouts, content organization for CMS integration, timeline cards, interactive storytelling, animation, visual presentation, and helping translate designs into Wix Harmony.

I collaborated with fellow designers, developers, and the nonprofit client, incorporating feedback into the design. The overall website refresh and final delivery belong to our team.

03 / THE PROCESS

From the first question
to the next iteration.

01 · Wireframing & exploration

I created wireframes and page layouts to explore how content could be presented across the Sankofa subsite and main homepage. Original Figma artifacts will be added to show the work directly.

02 · Leading the Sankofa design

As Sankofa subsite lead, I led its design work, including page layouts, timeline cards, and interactive storytelling elements. My focus was the subsite within the broader team website project.

03 · Homepage contributions

Alongside my Sankofa responsibilities, I contributed to the main homepage’s design and visual presentation. The homepage was a shared team effort; I do not claim sole authorship.

04 · Storytelling & CMS structure

I organized content for CMS integration and worked on timeline cards, animation, and visual storytelling. This brought content structure and presentation into the same design process.

05 · Client collaboration & iteration

I collaborated with designers, developers, and the nonprofit client, incorporating feedback into design iterations. Original annotated iterations will be added when available.

06 · From Figma to Wix Harmony

I helped translate designs into Wix Harmony and participated in the final project presentation and Demo Day. The completed website reflects the combined work of the design and development team.

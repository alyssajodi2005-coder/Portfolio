The overview

A little context.

Course Canvas is an educational technology project designed to help students organize coursework and academic responsibilities. It received Best Demo at CUNY Tech Prep Hacks.

Explore the live demo
02

The challenge

The problem worth solving.

A syllabus contains important information, but turning that information into an actionable plan can take effort. Course Canvas explores a more manageable way for students to navigate assignments, deadlines, and study planning.

Design goals & considerations

  1. Make academic responsibilities easier to find and navigate.
  2. Explore AI-assisted organization of syllabus information.
  3. Keep the path from course information to a plan understandable.

03 / THE PROCESS

From the first question
to the next iteration.

01 · Start with the semester

The intended users are students managing coursework and deadlines. No user interviews or usability testing have been provided.

02 · From syllabus to plan

The concept uses AI to turn syllabus information into planning tools such as assignments, deadlines, and study schedules. The live sign-in screen states that syllabi are sent to Gemini for parsing. The parsing pipeline and its accuracy have not been independently verified here.

03 · Organize the interface

The interface aims to make academic information easier to navigate. The live demo organizes courses as a semester diagram, with collision-week advisories and a workload forecast. Unconfirmed dates are visibly marked. The navigation separates semester information, planning, and study tools. The displayed counts are demo data, not impact metrics.
